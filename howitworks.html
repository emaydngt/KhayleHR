THE PROCESS

How KhayleHR Works

1

Collect Feedback

Employees submit feedback, pulse responses, or raise concerns through any device — anonymously or identified.

2

Analyze Data

AI-powered sentiment analysis aggregates responses into actionable insights and trend reports for HR leaders.

3

Communicate Back

Leaders acknowledge feedback and share updates, demonstrating responsiveness and building trust in the change process.

4

Drive Action

Assign tasks, track resolutions, and measure the impact of changes — creating a continuous improvement loop.
