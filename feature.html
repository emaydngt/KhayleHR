CORE FEATURES

Everything You Need to Manage Organizational Change

KhayleHR equips HR teams and leadership with tools to listen, respond, and lead change effectively.

Anonymous Feedback Channel

Employees can share honest feedback without fear, fostering a culture of psychological safety during IS transitions and organizational change.

Real-Time Analytics Dashboard

Track employee sentiment, engagement scores, and feedback trends with live charts to quickly identify resistance points in your IS rollout.

Change Announcements

Broadcast system updates, policy changes, and IS implementation milestones directly to employees with read receipts and engagement tracking.

Pulse Surveys

Launch targeted micro-surveys at any phase of the change process to gauge readiness, understanding, and adoption of new information systems.

Smart Notification System

Automated alerts notify managers when engagement drops or negative sentiment spikes, enabling proactive intervention before resistance escalates.

Structured Action Plans

Convert employee feedback into actionable improvement plans with assigned owners, deadlines, and progress tracking — closing the feedback loop.
